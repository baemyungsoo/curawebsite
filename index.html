Safety

A safety net behind every dose

cura cross-references Singapore's HSA drug registry against each family member's recorded allergies and existing medications before a dose is ever logged — and makes the result visible, not buried in a settings page.

cura is a careful companion, not a certified practitioner — it's built to catch common, well-established risks, and allows you to have a conversation with your qualified clinician for a final judgement.

Allergy conflict
Flags a medication that matches — or shares an active ingredient with — a recorded allergy.
Drug interaction
Warns when two active medications are a known-risk combination, down to minimum safe dose spacing.
Duplicate medication
Catches the same medicine, or drug class, added twice under two different brand names.

Why cura

Built from a kitchen-table problem

cura started the way a lot of real problems do — with a row of prescription bottles on a counter, each on its own frequency, and no easy way to tell at a glance what had been taken and what hadn't. Spreadsheets were too slow to update; paper got lost or out of date within a week.

cura was built to fix that for one household first — then shaped, alpha by alpha, into something other families managing multiple medications could rely on too.

Real-world evidence

Opt in to research. Earn rewards.

With your explicit, per-medication consent, cura can share anonymised adherence and efficacy data with pharmaceutical real-world-evidence (RWE) partners researching how treatments actually perform outside a clinical trial — and pass real value back to the family that contributed it.

Opt in, medication by medication

The same toggle behind cura's Medication Efficacy Survey — a deliberate, per-medication choice, never an account-wide default.

Anonymised, consent-first

Treatment consent and data-sharing consent stay legally separate under Singapore's PDPA — withdraw either, any time.

Earn rewards back

Every opted-in course builds toward rewards your family can put toward its own health or medication spending.

FAQ

Questions families ask us

Is my family's medication data private?

Yes. cura stores everything on-device in a local SQLite database — nothing leaves your phone by default. Research participation (see the Rewards section above) is a separate, explicit opt-in under Singapore's PDPA, kept apart from your everyday treatment data.

Does cura work without an internet connection?

Yes. cura is on-device first — schedules, dose logs and safety checks all run locally, so a spotty connection at home won't stop a dose from being tracked.

Is cura free to use?

Yes, cura is free to use, with no plans to put core tracking or safety checks behind a paywall.

Can more than one family member use the same cura app?

Yes — add every family member as their own profile, each with a colour, avatar, allergies and dose history, and switch between them in one tap.

Will cura catch every risk with my medications?

cura flags common, well-established allergy conflicts, drug interactions and duplicate medications — but it's a careful companion, not a certified practitioner. Always confirm a flagged risk with your pharmacist or doctor.

When is cura coming to iPhone?

cura is Android-first today, built and tested on-device there. An iOS version is on the roadmap above, alongside cloud sync and the Clinic Suite.
